Once you have learned the technique, you can re-tape your extensions yourself at home. You don't need to see a stylist for every correction: you just need the right materials and to follow the technique.

## What you'll need

- special tapes and double-sided extension tape;
- primers and other supplies;
- a professional tape remover;
- tools for sectioning hair and placing the attachments.

Tape, primer and remover are available in our [materials section](/catalog/materials).

When you buy hair, we send recorded video lessons on working with tapes. You can study the steps and come back to the videos whenever you need.

## How often to correct

Correction is individual and is done every 2–3 months. The exact timing depends on your individual physiology and how fast your own hair grows, as well as on how you care for it and where the tapes are placed.

Don't put it off until the tapes have grown out a lot or tangles start forming at the roots.
