Da Vinchi Hair is a brand from Tbilisi, Georgia. We sell quality hair and Tape-In extension systems: classic tapes and tapes with Root Imitation 1.0 and 2.0.

Besides retail sales, we supply stylists and salons wholesale and teach the Tape-In technique online and in person in Tbilisi.

The idea behind the brand is simple: you choose quality hair and materials once and get a system you can use again and again, with correct installation and care.
