Tape-In with root imitation is an advanced version of classic tape extensions. The core technique stays the same: the hair is attached with a wide tape that spreads the load evenly over your own hair. On top of that, a special root imitation makes the attachment point even less visible.

The main advantage of this technique is the most natural look at the roots. The imitation helps hide the tape and creates the impression that the extra hair grows right from the scalp. This matters most if you want to wear your hair down, change your parting and put your hair up.

There are two types of imitation, 1.0 and 2.0. Both hide the attachments inside the hair, but they differ in how the edge is finished, how complex they are to make, how long production takes and how much they cost. You also don't need imitation for all of your hair: different types of tape can be combined to get a beautiful result exactly where it's needed.

## Why imitation

A classic tape is about 4 cm wide and about 1 cm high. With certain head positions, styles or a strong gust of wind, the edge of the attachment can show, especially when the hair flies up and reveals the extension areas.

Unlike a regular classic tape, the imitation is built to hide the attachment completely inside the hair. This makes the extensions much less noticeable, even when the wind blows your hair around.

## Imitation 1.0: a neatly hidden attachment

Imitation 1.0 gives a neater, less visible attachment than a regular classic tape. Its construction hides most of the attachment inside the hair while keeping a tidy look.

Its key feature is a small visible edge of about 2–3 mm.

It's a great option if you want a neat, barely visible attachment and don't want to wait several weeks for it to be made.

- less visible than a classic tape;
- a small visible edge of about 2–3 mm;
- made in 5–7 days on average;
- 50 cm, 100 g of hair costs $390.

## Imitation 2.0: every hair worked by hand

Imitation 2.0 is a more complex technique with special attention to how the edge is finished.

The attachment is fully hand-stitched, and each hair is worked individually. This gives the neatest possible edge with no visible line.

It's the choice for those who care about the finest details and want the least visible attachment possible.

- fully hand-stitched;
- each hair worked individually;
- no visible edge;
- made in 3–4 weeks on average;
- 50 cm, 100 g of hair costs $520.

## Why Imitation 2.0 costs more

The difference in price comes mainly from how complex and time-consuming it is to make.

Imitation 1.0 is produced faster, while Imitation 2.0 takes much more handwork: each hair is worked individually and the edge of the attachment is shaped with great care. That extra time and handwork is what the final price reflects.

## Do I need imitation for all 100 grams?

No. In most cases it's enough to use imitation only where attachments could be seen or exposed by certain hairstyles. Usually these are:

- **the temples**, so attachments stay invisible around the face;
- **the crown**, especially if you like changing your parting or wearing your hair up high;
- **the lower back of the head**, if you often wear a high ponytail and want the attachments to be less visible.

The exact set of areas depends on how thick your own hair is, the layout and the hairstyles you wear most often.

### Combining different types of tape

Inner areas that are well covered by your own hair and the extensions don't need imitation. There, [classic tapes](/technology/tape-in) or [bio-tapes](/technology/bio-tape) can be used, depending on the texture and thickness of your own hair, the chosen technique and the load the attachments need to carry.

This way, one set of extensions can combine different types of tape: imitation in the visible areas and, everywhere else, the option that suits your hair best. The result is invisible attachments where it really matters, at a sensible overall cost.

## Which imitation to choose

**Imitation 1.0** if you want a neat, barely visible attachment in a shorter time.

**Imitation 2.0** if having no visible edge is essential and you want the most meticulous handwork.

You don't have to choose one technique for your whole head. We can put together a combination of imitation, classic tapes and bio-tapes based on your hair and the result you want.
